# Privacy Policy — Folio Workbench

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Folio Workbench is developed by Sourabh Tilawat. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Your files stay on your device
Every tool in Folio Workbench — merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing, signing, redacting, OCR and the rest — runs entirely on your device. Your documents, images and signatures are never uploaded to any server, and the developer never receives them.

## Information we collect
The developer collects nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics, and your documents are never uploaded.

The free version of the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only part of the app that sends data off your device (see "Advertising" below). Purchases are processed by Google Play.

## Advertising (free version only)
The free Android version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location derived from it, information about your device, and how you interact with the ads. Google processes this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its [advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads); the developer does not receive it. Your files are never shared with the ad network.

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first and you can change your choice anytime under **Get Pro › Ad privacy choices**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's **Settings › Privacy › Ads**.
- Buying Folio Pro removes all ads, and the app then stops loading the ad SDK's ads.

## Purchases (Folio Pro)
On Android you can buy Folio Pro, a one-time unlock, through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; the developer never sees them. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Folio Pro and remembers the answer on your device so Pro works offline. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What is stored on your device
To save you time, the app remembers your option choices, the signature you last created and whether Folio Pro is unlocked. These stay only in the app's private storage on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

## Permissions
- **Internet:** used to show ads in the free version and to download optional OCR language packs the first time you choose a language that is not built in. Your files are never sent.
- **Advertising ID:** used by Google AdMob in the free version, as described above.
- **Storage (Android 9 and older only):** used to save the files you create to your Downloads folder.

Files you choose with the file picker or share to the app are opened only to perform the task you asked for.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
